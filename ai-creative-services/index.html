The workflow

Creative thinking first. Tools second.

Every project starts with the outcome you need. We agree on the direction, create and refine the assets, then prepare deliverables for the intended channel.

Step 01

Brief & references

Share the product, audience, usage, must-have details and brand guidelines or reference visuals.

Step 02

Creative direction

Agree on the concept, visual approach, aspect ratios and deliverables before production.

Step 03

Create & refine

Build AI-assisted outputs and review the selected direction against the brief, with revisions agreed upfront.

Step 04

Final delivery

Receive approved assets in the agreed formats for your website, social channels or campaign.

Frequently asked questions

AI creative services FAQs

What does AI creative production include?

Depending on the brief, it can include AI-assisted product visuals, campaign imagery, short-form videos and social ad creatives. Scope, formats and revisions are agreed before work starts.

Do I need to provide product photos?

For product-specific work, reference photos, packaging details and brand guidelines are helpful for keeping outputs aligned with the real product and its identity.

Can content follow my existing brand guidelines?

Yes. Share your logos, colors, typography, visual references and required product details during briefing so the creative direction can account for them.

How much does an AI creative project cost?

Pricing depends on deliverables, complexity, number of variations and editing required. Share your brief to receive a scoped proposal.

Do you work with clients outside Delhi?

KJS Creative is based in New Delhi and can discuss remote projects with brands and teams across India.
